Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains when you're eligible for a refund on a PropertyIQ purchase, and how to request one. PropertyIQ has three distinct types of paid products, and the refund terms are different for each because the products themselves work differently — please check which one applies to your purchase.

1. Instant Property Score reports (one-time purchase)

A Standard Report is generated and delivered to you immediately upon payment. Because the report is instantly and fully delivered digital content, purchases are generally non-refundable once the report has been generated.

We will refund a report purchase if:

  • You were charged but the report was never generated due to a technical failure on our end
  • You were charged more than once for the same report (duplicate charge)
  • The report is materially incomplete or clearly incorrect due to a system error, not a data-coverage limitation we already disclosed (e.g. a city we don't yet have comparable data for)

2. Insight Add-on (one-time purchase)

Same standard as above — the Insight Add-on unlocks similar-property suggestions instantly upon purchase. Refundable only for a technical failure to deliver, or a duplicate charge, requested within 14 days of purchase.

3. Construction Studio subscriptions (Starter / Pro / Unlimited)

These are recurring monthly subscriptions. Because they grant ongoing access rather than a single delivered item, the terms are different from the one-time purchases above:

  • First-time subscribers: if you're unhappy with your first month, contact us within 7 days of your initial charge for a full refund of that first payment. This applies once per customer.
  • Renewal charges: renewal payments for a subscription you continued using are not refunded — cancel any time before your next billing date to avoid being charged for the next period. Cancelling does not refund the current period, but you keep access until it ends.
  • Billing errors: a duplicate charge, a charge after you'd already cancelled, or a charge that doesn't match your actual plan will always be refunded in full, with no time limit on reporting it.

4. How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are issued back to your original payment method through Dodo Payments, our payment processor. Processing typically takes 5-10 business days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank or card issuer — this part is outside our control once the refund is issued.

5. How to request a refund

Email us using the address below with your account email and, if you have it, the payment confirmation or invoice ID. We aim to respond to every refund request within 3 business days.
