Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This explains what information PropertyIQ collects, why, and who else sees it. PropertyIQ is built and operated by an independent developer, not a large company with a dedicated privacy team — this policy describes plainly what actually happens with your data rather than generic legal boilerplate.

2. Third-party services we use

We rely on the following services to operate PropertyIQ. Each only receives the specific data needed for its job:

  • Dodo Payments — processes all payments. Handles your card details directly; we never see them.
  • Google Gemini — used as a fallback when importing property details from a listing URL you provide, if the page's own structured data isn't enough.
  • Tavily — used to search for real, current infrastructure news for a city you're viewing on Neighborhood Insights.
  • LocationIQ — provides maps and address autocomplete on Neighborhood Insights.
  • Resend — sends login codes and transactional emails.
  • Google Translate — auto-translates the page for visitors outside India, using only the page's own text.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data to anyone.
  • We don't show third-party ads.
  • We don't share your property details or email with other users, except a property you explicitly share with named collaborators (a Studio Unlimited feature) or a "Should I Buy This?" challenge link you choose to send someone.

4. How long we keep data

Your account and saved properties are kept as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, email us using the address below and we'll do so within 14 days, except where we're required to retain payment records for tax/accounting purposes.

5. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • See what data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and data (subject to the retention note above)

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that materially affects how your data is handled, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
